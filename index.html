    Privacy

    Privacy notice

    Draft for review by a qualified UK legal adviser before launch (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

    What we collect

    Account details (name, email and phone), listings you submit, enquiries and viewing requests, payment records and your communication preferences with consent timestamps.

    Private documents

    Identity and ownership documents are stored privately, are never published with a listing and are only seen by you and authorised reviewers. Access to them is logged.

    Your choices

    You can update your details, change notification preferences and request deactivation or deletion from your profile at any time.

    Terms

    Terms of use

    Draft for legal review.

    Listings

    You may only advertise a property you own or are authorised to advertise. Every listing is reviewed; we may request changes, reject or suspend a listing.

    Paid plans

    Price, duration, limits, renewal, cancellation and refund terms are shown before you pay. Paid features activate only once payment is confirmed. Recurring billing is always disclosed and needs your authorisation.

    Our role

    Nestora is a marketplace and does not act as an estate agent or broker unless separately agreed.
