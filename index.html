    About Nestora

    A marketplace built on reviewed listings

    Nestora connects buyers, tenants and property owners across the United Kingdom. Individual owners list their homes and land; agencies and property management companies showcase their portfolios.

    Every property goes through its own review before it appears anywhere public — search, the map, company pages or links shared online. If a listing is suspended, sold, let or expires, it leaves search straight away.

    We host listings and connect people; we don't act as your estate agent or broker. Always view a property and take independent advice before you commit.

    What we collect

    Account details (name, email and phone), listings you submit, enquiries and viewing requests, payment records and your communication preferences with consent timestamps.

    Private documents

    Identity and ownership documents are stored privately, are never published with a listing and are only seen by you and authorised reviewers. Access to them is logged.

    You can update your details, change notification preferences and request deactivation or deletion from your profile at any time.
